# PriceMonkey: video script (v2)

Spoken by one of us, straight to camera, like Louis Rossman: first person, fast, a bit annoyed, real
receipts on screen. Under 2:00 (about 330 words at his pace). [SCREEN] = what we cut to.

---

**[0:00] Host to camera.**

Hey everybody, how's it going? Hope you're having a lovely day.

So last night I tried to buy a PS5 Pro. Used. Should be easy, right?

[SCREEN: the OLX listing, 1,600 zł]
First listing I find: sixteen hundred złoty. That's about three hundred and sixty euros. For a PS5 Pro.

[SCREEN: scroll down, another PS5 Pro, 3,100 zł]
Same page. Another one. Thirty-one hundred.

So either this guy is the nicest person in Poland, or it's a scam. No reviews. Listed yesterday. Twelve
hundred people already clicked on it.

**[0:22] Host.**

And I'm not saying online shopping is a little annoying. I'm saying it's built to make you pay more than you
think.

[SCREEN: a US shop, $699.95]
The tag says six hundred and thirty-three euros. It's a US shop, so add a hundred and sixty-two in import VAT,
and shipping is "calculated at the next step."

[SCREEN: comparison site, "Sponsored" x3]
The comparison site? First three results are ads.

[SCREEN: tabs piling up, 14 tabs]
Fourteen tabs later you buy the one you saw first, and you still don't know if it was a good price.

Good job, internet.

**[0:45] Host.**

So we built a thing. It's called PriceMonkey. Yes, there's a monkey. He does the boring part.

[SCREEN: PriceMonkey, type "sony a7 iii body"]
You type what you want, like you'd say it to a friend. And it reads the shops for you. Tori, Kleinanzeigen,
Marktplaats, Vinted, refurbishers, normal stores. All at once.

[SCREEN: the live log, sites lighting up]
That's Context.dev doing the heavy lifting. It searches the web, opens every page and turns each listing into
clean data. We never wrote a scraper for a single shop.

**[1:08]** [SCREEN: offers land on the line, then it flips to "what you actually pay"]

Then every offer lands on one line, and the line flips to what you'd actually pay at your door. The
six-thirty-three one? Seven ninety-five, and it might not even ship here. The used one in Finland? Eight
twenty-five. All in. Done.

[SCREEN: the PS5 Pro listing, "Set aside: too good to be true"]
And that three-hundred-and-sixty-euro PS5 Pro? Set aside. Not ranked. And it tells you why.

**[1:32] Host to camera.**

Now the part I actually care about. If you buy through us, we might earn a fee. We will never sell a better
spot. No sponsored results. The best real price wins. If we ever take money to rank a shop higher, you have my
permission to make a video about us.

[SCREEN: agent view, the same pick as JSON]
And if you're building an AI agent, it gets the exact same answer. Same line, same rules.

**[1:52] Host. End card: pricemonkey.eu · powered by Context.dev**

PriceMonkey. Ask the monkey.

That's it for today, and as always, I hope you learned something.

## Notes for recording

- Everything on screen is real: the OLX listings are from our saved pages (14 Nov), the Sony numbers are from
  the recorded hunt ($699.95 → €633 tag → €795 at the door; tori.fi €825), and the "set aside" reason is what
  PriceMonkey actually shows. Record the app from the replays so the numbers match.
- Say it in your own words. If a line feels stiff out loud, change it. The point is the feeling: annoyed,
  honest, on the buyer's side.
- Keep the monkey small: one line ("he does the boring part") and the end card.
- Do not say: "it buys for you", "reads any website", live speeds in milliseconds, Exa/Oxylabs/Gemini.
- Hard limit 2:00. Public YouTube/Vimeo link; switch the project to FINAL before Sunday 10:00.
