## Tab One (lock the cut at 1:52, hard cap 2:00, about 249 written words / about 265 said out loud)

**Voice:** one guy at his desk showing a friend his screen. Dry and fed up. He speeds up when he's irritated and never performs a pause. No music, just room tone.

**Layout after the cold open:** a vertical split.
- LEFT = "the normal way": a real Chrome recording on his Mac. A tab counter and a stopwatch are burned into the top left, with a tiny "real time" label beside the stopwatch. The footage may be sped up, but the stopwatch always shows true elapsed time.
- RIGHT = PriceMonkey replaying the recorded hunt `?q=sony%20a7%20iii%20body`. It sits at 40% brightness until 0:36. In its top-right corner is THE COUNTER, a plain monospace number that tracks only the allenscamera.net offer and only goes up. It starts at "tag: $699.95". A small permanent label reads "recorded hunt, partly cached".
- When RIGHT goes full screen, LEFT shrinks to a picture-in-picture in the bottom left with its stopwatch still running. The race never leaves the screen.
