## TABS RACE v7 (target 1:50, hard cap 2:00, 267 written / about 275 spoken words, no music)

**Layout from 0:04:** vertical split.
- LEFT, labelled "the normal way": a real Chrome screen recording on a Mac. Two counters are burned into the top-left corner: the tab count and a real-time stopwatch that starts at tab one. The footage can be sped up, but the stopwatch always shows true elapsed time.
- RIGHT: the PriceMonkey replay at `?q=sony%20a7%20iii%20body`. A permanent label, big enough to read on a phone: [TEXT: recorded hunt, partly cached]. This label replaces the old spoken disclaimer.
- All edit overlays (counters, `tabs: 1`, captions) use one burned-in style, white mono on a black chip, so nobody mistakes them for the app's own interface. Everything else on RIGHT is the app exactly as it renders.
- When RIGHT goes full screen, LEFT shrinks to a picture-in-picture in the bottom-left corner and its counters keep running. The race never leaves the screen.
- The VO never says how many tabs or minutes. Those numbers appear only on the counters.
- Delivery: say the run-ons (0:18, 0:25, 1:11) in one breath, a bit fast and a bit annoyed. Say the short lines flat. Say "A7 III" as "A seven mark three".

Two fact fixes on top of the critique:
1) The mpb line keeps 'Probably', because the app's own flag says 'probably another item's price'. Saying 'That's some other item's price' would overclaim.

Type 699.95 × 0.90481 × 1.255 instead: it gives 794.82 and matches the receipt exactly. Keep the ECB page flash under about 10 frames so nobody compares it with that day's real rate. If you'd rather use the real rate on the day, don't hold the PiP on the result at 'My calculator'.
