## Final judges
- judge1 6.5: It's Tab One plus more, and the 'more' is the problem. It has the best single line in the batch (permission to pay them), a sharp 'A lazy price site puts that on top' contrast, an accurate 'almost half the pages get thrown out', and the seller-message feature. But at 277 words, four takes and two price sequences (+€161.50 import VAT, then €20/mo vs €9/mo), it falls back into the v1 sin of cramming numbers. A viewer won't hold €9.16/mo against €20.49/mo after resale in 12 seconds. The one-word face-cam 'Sure.' cut is good. The 'It was tab one' payoff is weaker here than in Tab One because the per-month detour came first. Cut the resale-per-month beat and it climbs.
- judge2 7.5: The most complete script and the closest to what the owner asked for. It opens on a problem told in the first person (the Chrome tab hunt, the calculator, shipping never answered), then reaches the tool at about 0:34 and lands on a real twist: tab one was right all along, and you couldn't know without checking every tab. Every number is in the bible: €825, $699.95, +€161.50, €794.82, the €239 mpb flag, 23 pages set aside, €20/mo vs €9.16/mo, and the seller message. It discloses the cached replay in a caption, says Context.dev is used for both search and reading, and says 'never buys a better spot' plainly. 'A lazy price site puts that on top' aims at a specific practice. Weaknesses: Context.dev is only a caption and a voice line, and nothing on screen shows the extraction. The per-month resale numbers come late and add load. Real-time and watches never appear. With four takes and many cuts, the edit has to be tight to hold 1:49.
- judge3 6.5: It has the same relatable tab hunt and the same great tab-one payoff, plus the best single joke of the six (asking permission to pay). But it's busier. The "normal way" chunk runs 26 seconds, and the back half piles on €20/mo vs €9.16/mo resale math, a seller-message feature, "23 set aside" and "half the pages thrown out". By then I'm checking how much is left on the scrub bar. The cut to face cam for "Sure." is good. "Talk fast" is doing a lot of work to make this fit. Cut the per-month comparison or the seller-message bit and it beats rant-plus-race.
- judge4 7.5: It has the same strong spine as Tab One and better product proof: the 23 set-aside pages, the +€161.50 import VAT line, an honest 'partly cached' caption, and the suggested message to the seller. Every fact checks out against the bible, and the four takes are easy to plan. My problem is density. At 277 words in 1:49, the 1:17 beat brings in resale and per-month costs (€20 vs €9) with about four seconds to explain them. That is the v1 sin of cramming numbers in, and viewers will lose the thread right where the verdict should land. The one-word 'Sure.' face-cam insert is fun but costs a camera reset for half a second of footage. 'Find the shop pages and read them' is accurate. Like Tab One, it never shows the free-versus-paid read. I'd cut the resale sentence and the seller-message shot, and then it beats Tab One.
